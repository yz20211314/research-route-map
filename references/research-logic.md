# Research logic rules

## Extraction order

Read in this order: objective/question; object/unit/boundary; theory/context; data/sample/evidence; substantive work packages; matched methods; variables/indicators/criteria; mechanism or relationship; validation; outputs and translation.

## Route selection

Select `route_mode` first and a dominant route archetype second. Select a `domain_profile` and use it as an applicability checklist.

## Relation semantics

Use relation types precisely:

- `sequence` — one activity is performed after another;
- `causal` — the source is claimed or hypothesized to cause/influence the target;
- `support` — theory, data, method, indicator, or validation supports a work package;
- `parallel` — sibling activities occur without implied order;
- `decision` — a criterion selects a branch or progression gate;
- `feedback` — a later result informs revision of an earlier activity;
- `containment` — a child belongs to a parent or group.

Do not use a directed arrow for mere co-membership or visual alignment. Preserve uncertainty in labels and in source evidence.

## Research-process stage construction

Construct 3–6 stages when supported. Every retained stage should state:

- objective;
- inputs;
- two to four substantive tasks;
- relation among tasks;
- method/data mapped to each task;
- indicator, milestone, or decision;
- validation and handoff.

The final task is not automatically a milestone. Designate a stage output only when the confirmed framework identifies one.

For new semantic research-process projects, distinguish the visible roles:

- `groups[].short_label` is the concise research thinking, such as “理论构建” or “机制检验”;
- the primary content lane explains which concrete data or technique performs which task, how it is checked, and what it produces;
- the method lane summarizes only broad research-method categories such as “文献分析法” or “案例分析法”.

Do not create a separate thinking lane. Do not move datasets, models, variables, indicators, or validation details into the summary method rail.

## Other route modes

- `research-framework`: use alignment, containment, and explicit supported relationships. A stage count is a density target, not proof of chronology.
- `technology-roadmap`: organize by time horizon and capability/technology layers; show maturity/KPI, dependencies, gates, and risks.
- `study-flow`: follow the applicable domain flow; preserve counts/status and reasons rather than converting the diagram into generic work packages.

## Compression

Compress wording without changing modality or certainty. Preserve “可能”, “拟”, “探索”, “验证”, “预期”, and “相关”. Do not turn association into causation or a proposal into a completed result.

## Support lanes

- the research-thinking rail answers “what concise logic organizes this research unit”;
- content answers “what work is performed, with which concrete data/technique, and toward which result”;
- the summary method rail answers only “which broad research-method categories are used”;
- theory/context answers “under which assumptions”;
- indicator/outcome answers “what is measured, decided, or delivered”.

Connect every method, dataset, theory, indicator, and validation node to the exact work package or milestone it supports in the graph.

Rendering is separate:

- `aligned` method rail: show cards without support connectors;
- `mapped`: show selected solid support connectors;
- `hidden`: hide cards while preserving nodes and graph edges.

## Visual information hierarchy

Use three levels:

1. macro stage/layer/time horizon;
2. work package, decision, or capability block;
3. explicit child items, method/data, indicator, validation, or outcome.

Do not render a macro group as a single node when the framework contains internal work packages.
